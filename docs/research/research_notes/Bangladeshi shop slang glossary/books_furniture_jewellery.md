## 1. How are items named, and which attribute words matter for lookups? (item vocabulary per business type)

### Takeaway
Items are named by a short everyday noun (boi, khata, khat, almari, churi, har) plus 2-4 attribute words that decide which row you hit: **class/subject/publisher/edition** (books), **size in feet, material/wood, brand, set-vs-piece** (furniture), **karat, weight in vori/ana/roti or gram, category (ornament type), and brand/model** (jewellery/watches). Sources confirm the core nouns and many attributes (BAJUS karat classes, class names, bed sizes written "6/7", wood names, Hatil sizes); the Banglish spellings are my construction.

### Cited Findings
**Books and stationery**
- Rokomari top-level categories are written in Bangla script: বই (books), ই-বুক, ইসলামি বই, একাডেমিক (academic), স্টেশনারি (stationery), কিডস জোন, and "ভর্তি, নিয়োগ ও চাকরির প্রস্তুতি পরীক্ষার বই" (admission/recruitment/job-prep exam books); product names also carry English tokens like "HSC", "Admission Test Preparation", "2026" and "parallel text" — [Rokomari](https://www.rokomari.com)
- Retail talk about guides uses: গাইড / গাইড বই (guide), মডেল গাইড / মডেল বই (model guide/book), জোড়া বই / জোড়া গাইড (a paired English-2nd-paper + Bangla-2nd-paper set sold as one), প্যাকেট (bundle), অবিক্রীত (unsold stock), ছাড় (discount), বিক্রয় প্রতিনিধি (publisher sales rep), পুস্তক ব্যবসায়ী (book trader). Class names are written in ordinal words: ষষ্ঠ, সপ্তম, অষ্টম, নবম, দশম শ্রেণি. Publishers named in the report: Lecture Prokashani, Panjeri Prokashani. A Class 8 English+Bangla 2nd-paper pair was 1,060 taka (Mar 2025, Bhairab) — [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/district/lum6249tku)
- Paper is sold as "ream" in English listings ("500 Sheets", "A4 80 GSM / 70 GSM") — e.g. Papertech A4 80 GSM 500 sheets Tk 449 on Rokomari ([Rokomari](https://www.rokomari.com/product/283606/papertech-a4-offset-paper-80-gsm-500-sheets)); Chaldal spells it "1 rim" (Bashundhara A4 80 GSM, ৳520) ([Chaldal](https://chaldal.com/bashundhara-a4-size-paper-gsm-80)). So "rim/rim/ream" are all in use and 1 ream = 500 sheets in current retail listings.

**Furniture and homeware**
- Wood names (Bangla script) with use: সেগুন (teak; dearest; bed, dining table, almirah), মেহগনি (mahogany; bed, almirah, dressing table, bookshelf; warps if not seasoned), গামারি (light local wood; cabinets), গর্জন (strong, cheap; door frames, beds, tables), ওক/ম্যাপল (imported), plywood/MDF/particle board ("ইঞ্জিনিয়ার্ড উড"), সিজনিং (seasoning), ভিনিয়ার (veneer), কেবিনেট, বুকশেলফ. The article says MDF/particle board are at risk in monsoon — [Daily Star Bangla](https://bangla.thedailystar.net/business/news-3956856)
- Bikroy bed listings write sizes as "৫/৭", "৬/৭", "৪/৭" (feet x feet) and "৬×৭ ফুট কিং সাইজ", with "কুইন সাইজ" cheaper; one teak king frame listed at about 38,500 and queen about 34,500 — [Bikroy bed frames](https://bikroy.com/bn/20-bed-frames) (listing/asking prices only, not market prices). Bikroy furniture categories include cabinets and cupboards, dining sets, bookcases and shelving; individual ads use খাট, আলমারি, ড্রেসিং টেবিল, সোফা সেট, ডাইনিং টেবিল, শোকেস, and brand names such as Partex.
- Hatil mattress page lists subcategories "King Size Mattress", "Queen Size Mattress", "Semi Double Mattress" and the word "Toshok" — [Hatil mattress](https://hatil.com/mattress). Hatil showroom pages advertise "flexible EMI" on furniture; a Hatil bed page says 3-day return with a 10% service charge, excluding customised/broken-set/fabric items (per search summary of Hatil pages; I did not read each page).

**Jewellery and watches**
- BAJUS publishes four rate classes per bhori: ২২ ক্যারেট, ২১ ক্যারেট, ১৮ ক্যারেট, সনাতন পদ্ধতির সোনা ("traditional-method gold") — [TBS Bangla, 2 Feb 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangla/bangladesh/news-details-447521); same four classes in English on 21 Jul 2026: 22K Tk 2,21,441; 21K Tk 2,11,468; 18K Tk 1,81,608; traditional Tk 1,48,366 per bhori — [TBS, 21 Jul 2026](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/bajus-raises-gold-price-tk1633-bhori-1493736)
- Ornament names seen in a Bangladeshi press piece on buying gold jewellery: চোকার (choker), টিকলি (tikli, moon-and-star motif), মটর হার (motor har), "প্রেম ফাঁস" (prem phas chain/bangle), অর্ধচন্দ্রাকার কানের দুল — [Prothom Alo shopping piece](https://www.prothomalo.com/lifestyle/shopping/l5k6t5x84a) (from a search summary; I did not open the article).
- Titan/Fastrack/Casio/Rolex listings, new and used, exist on Bikroy; one ad says it comes with "রিসিট এবং ওয়ারেন্টি" — [Bikroy Fastrack](https://bikroy.com/bn/watches/fastrack). Titan has opened exclusive brand/service stores in Bangladesh (6th store announced) — [Titan Company](https://titancompany.in/news/titan-opens-6th-exclusive-brand-store-bangladesh)
- Diamond ornaments: BAJUS's 2023 guidelines require a cash memo stating diamond colour and carat, and a certificate above "50 cents" (i.e. 0.50 ct) — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/bajus-rolls-out-new-jewellery-guidelines)

## 2. Units, measures, packaging and traditional units: exact meanings and safe conversions

### Takeaway
In Bangladesh gold: **1 vori (ভরি) = 11.664 g = 16 ana = 96 roti/rati**; 1 ana = 0.729 g; 1 roti = 0.1215 g; one source gives 1 roti = 10 point so 1 point = 0.01215 g (single-source, unverified). Vori and tola are the same weight in Bangladeshi use (British-India tola = 11.6638038 g). Dozen=12, gross=144, ream=500 sheets (modern), gonda/kuri/pon/dista are traditional counts I could not source well.

### Cited Findings
- 1 ভরি = ১১.৬৬৪ গ্রাম; 16 আনা = 1 ভরি; 6 রতি = 1 আনা; 22K price is quoted for that 11.664 g — [Bangla Wikipedia, ভরি](https://bn.wikipedia.org/wiki/ভরি)
- BAJUS notices quote "ভরি" as 11.664 g — [TBS Bangla](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangla/bangladesh/news-details-447521)
- Tola under the 1833 British-Indian standard = 180 troy grains = 11.6638038 g (3/8 troy ounce); Bengal-Presidency chain 1 tola = 12 masha, 1 masha = 8 ratti (=> 96 ratti); the 10-tola bar is the most commonly traded bar in Bangladesh and neighbours — [Wikipedia, Tola](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tola_(unit))
- A Bangla news explainer gives 1 ভরি = 16 আনা = 96 রতি = 960 পয়েন্ট, 1 আনা = 0.729 g, 1 রতি = 0.1215 g, 1 পয়েন্ট = 0.01215 g, and prices per ana/roti by dividing the vori price by 16 / 96 (example: at Tk 2,34,621 per vori of 22K, 1 ana about Tk 14,664 and 1 roti about Tk 2,444) — [teachers.gov.bd mirror](https://teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/849243) (seen only via search summary, single source for "point"; (uncertain) on point).
- Indian ratti is quoted as about 0.12125 g, slightly different from 0.1215 g (West Bengal/India note) — same search summary as above.
- An Indian bank blog's Bangla page says 1 ভরি ≈ 11.66 g but also lists "1 pavan = 1 bhori = 8 g" for some Indian states, i.e. regional definitions differ — [IIFL Bangla](https://www.iifl.com/bn/blogs/gold-loans/gold-weight-measures-used-in-different-indian-states) (search summary; I did not open it; treat as an India-only warning).

Safe-conversion rules:
1. Always carry weight internally in **grams** (or milligrams); convert vori/ana/roti on input and output.
2. Parse mixed weights like "২ ভরি ৫ আনা ৩ রতি" = 2×11.664 + 5×0.729 + 3×0.1215 = 23.328 + 3.645 + 0.3645 = 27.3375 g. (Arithmetic from the table, not from a source.)
3. Price of an item = weight(vori) × rate-per-vori(class) + making charge (+ VAT if the rate excludes it). 1 ana price = vori price / 16; 1 roti = vori price / 96 [S teachers.gov.bd summary].
4. Carat (gem) is a mass; karat (gold) is a purity. Never treat them as one field.

## 5. Ambiguous, regional or slang terms that cause errors (when to ask a follow-up)

### Takeaway
The riskiest traps are unit-related (vori/tola/gram, ratti variants, point), purity-naming (21K vs 22K, "traditional"), homophones (ঘড়ি/ভরি, রতি/রুটি, কড়াই/কড়ই, নগদ cash/Nagad), and context nouns (খাতা notebook/ledger, বাকি owed/left, কাল yesterday/tomorrow).

### Cited Findings
- Vori in Bangladesh/West Bengal: 11.664 g; Indian ratti 0.12125 vs 0.1215 g; some Indian states define "pavan/bhori" as 8 g — [Bangla Wikipedia](https://bn.wikipedia.org/wiki/ভরি), [IIFL Bangla](https://www.iifl.com/bn/blogs/gold-loans/gold-weight-measures-used-in-different-indian-states), [teachers.gov.bd](https://teachers.gov.bd/blog/details/849243) (secondary summaries).
- Bangladesh publishes 22K, 21K, 18K and "সনাতন পদ্ধতি" rates as four distinct lines per vori; the "traditional" line is far cheaper (about Tk 1.48 lakh vs Tk 2.21 lakh for 22K on 21 Jul 2026) — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/bajus-raises-gold-price-tk1633-bhori-1493736). The 2023 guidelines banned making/selling traditional ornaments but BAJUS rates still list the class in 2026 — [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/trade/bajus-rolls-out-new-jewellery-guidelines). I did not find a source defining "সনাতন পদ্ধতি" purity; one summary said purity is not stated in sources.

- Bed size shown "6/7", "5/7", "4/7" with "king/queen" labels in Bikroy ads; Hatil lists King, Queen and Semi Double for mattress — [Bikroy](https://bikroy.com/bn/20-bed-frames), [Hatil mattress](https://hatil.com/mattress).

## 6. Seasonal and demand words (wedding season, new academic year, boi mela, Eid, Pohela Boishakh)

### Takeaway
Demand is strongly seasonal, and queries often read "ei seasone", "eid er age", "boishakh e". Only a few seasonal facts were found in sources; the rest is [K] and should be tested with a shop.

### Cited Findings
- Guide-book sellers say new-year guides typically reach the market in February; Class 8 model-guide pairs were delayed to March 2025 because board books were late — [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/district/lum6249tku) (the February claim comes from a search summary of an Ittefaq/Prothom Alo report).
- Government stance in 2026: note-guides phased out, "sohayok boi" lists required — [Prothom Alo](https://www.prothomalo.com/education/gvqlexvmjp) (search summary).
- BAJUS rates change many times a month (reported 54 adjustments so far in 2026 as of the summary) — via the WebSearch summary of [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/1478916); gold rate lines should always show a timestamp.
